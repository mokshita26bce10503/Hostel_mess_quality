# Problem Statement

Hostel students frequently face issues related to food quality, hygiene, quantity, variety, and food wastage.

There is often no organized system to collect student feedback and analyze these issues systematically.

This project provides a platform for students to submit food quality feedback, register complaints, record food waste information, and vote for preferred dishes. The collected data is analyzed to generate reports that help improve hostel mess services.

# Objectives

- Collect student feedback on meals
- Monitor complaints regarding food services
- Analyze food waste
- Identify popular dishes
- Generate analytical reports
- Improve student satisfaction

# Target Users

- Students
- Hostel Wardens
- Mess Administrators

# Scope

- Food Quality Assessment
- Complaint Management
- Waste Monitoring
- Dish Voting
- Analytics and Reporting
